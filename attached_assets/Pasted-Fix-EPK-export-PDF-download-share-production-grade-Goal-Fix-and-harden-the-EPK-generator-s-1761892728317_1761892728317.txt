B. Worker & heavy tasks

packages/worker/epk-worker.js (Node script)

Accept jobs via IPC or watch a job queue file/DB

Job types: renderEpkHtmlToPdf, composeStamp, generateAssetsPackage

Use streaming for files (fs.createWriteStream) and sharp streaming when compositing

Use puppeteer-core to render HTML -> PDF:

Launch browserless or puppeteer-core with executablePath from env if available

Use page.setContent(html, { waitUntil: 'networkidle0' }) with a small safe timeout

Use page.pdf({ format: 'A4', printBackground:true, preferCSSPageSize:true }) streaming to file. Avoid in-memory buffers.

If puppeteer not available, fallback to:

pdfkit to assemble pages from images + text OR

html-pdf-node or wkhtmltopdf (document requirement)

Add concurrency control via p-limit or internal semaphore

packages/worker/queue.js

Minimal durable queue: jobs written to ./.local/state/epk-jobs.json (append-only) or use a simple SQLite file. Worker must atomically pick and update job status.
